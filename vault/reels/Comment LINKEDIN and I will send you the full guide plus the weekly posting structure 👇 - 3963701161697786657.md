Comment LINKEDIN and I will send you the full guide plus the weekly posting structure 👇

Recruiters were searching for someone like me. My profile just was not showing up.

I rebuilt it in 30 minutes with 4 Claude prompts. Here they are, in order.

▫️Audit what is weak

"I am targeting [role, e.g. Marketing Manager, remote]. Here is my headline: [paste]. My About: [paste]. My latest experience: [paste]. Score each out of 10 for clarity and how well it attracts recruiters. Tell me exactly what is weak or vague."

▫️Rewrite your headline

"Based on the weaknesses you found, rewrite my headline. Say what I do and who I help, not just my title. Give me 3 versions, one direct, one personal, one results focused."

▫️Rewrite your About

"Rewrite my About so it reads like a real person, not a formal bio. Open with what I do and who I help. Add 2 to 3 real achievements with numbers. End with what I am open to."

▫️Turn your week into a post

"Here is a real thing from my week: [describe it roughly]. Turn it into a short, honest LinkedIn post in my voice. Conversational, not corporate."

Fixing the profile got me found. Posting kept me visible. That combination got me 7 remote interviews.
