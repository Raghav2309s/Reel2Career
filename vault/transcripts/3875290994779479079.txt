This thing I am holding in my hands should not exist. It is one of the most unique, one of the most special, one of the rarest documents ever produced by a human being. It was never intended for publication. It is the secret, private thoughts of the most powerful man in the world. I'm talking about Marcus Aurelius' meditations. 2000 years ago, the emperor of Rome sat down and poured himself out onto the pages of a journal. He reviewed his values, his principles, the lessons he'd been taught in the crucible of life and somehow, miracle of miracles, this work survives. And it's a book that changed my life and has changed the life of millions of people in the intervening centuries.
