I have been at Amazon for more than five years and I've taken multiple rounds of interviews. And today I'm sharing what are the resume tips that get shortlisted in tech companies? First of all, let's make it clear. One resume does not fit all the companies, all the job roles. So make sure that you go through the job descriptions very, very deliberately and optimize your resume according to the job description job role and include the keywords which are mentioned in the job description. Half of your work is done there. Secondly, many companies are using AI to shortlist resumes these days. So make sure your resume is also JNAI optimized. Apart from that, mention your projects. In any tech role, what matters more than college or CGPA is what type of projects you have done. So make sure you mention the relevant projects that you have done according to the job role you are applying and also link your Githa profile.
